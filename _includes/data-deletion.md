Arrow Cube does not use accounts, so there is no account to sign in to or close. To ask us to delete the information linked to your game install:

1. In the game, open **Settings** and tap **Contact Us**. An email to **support-arrowcube@rubiconperform.com** opens with your player ID already in it.
2. Add **Delete my data** to the message and send it. We need the player ID to find your information, because we do not know your name or email address.

What happens next:

- Within 30 days we delete or anonymize the information linked to that player ID, and ask the partners that hold it on our behalf to do the same, as described in our [privacy policy](../privacy/).
- Purchase records we must keep by law (for example, for tax) are kept only as long as the law requires.
- Uninstalling the game deletes what is stored on your phone, but not what was already sent to us or our partners. Email us for that before you uninstall, because a new install gets a new player ID.

Advertising partners hold their data under your phone's advertising ID, which you control:

- **Android:** reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your phone.
- **iPhone:** allow or stop tracking for Arrow Cube in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Arrow Cube is published by Rubicon Performance LLC. Questions: support-arrowcube@rubiconperform.com.
