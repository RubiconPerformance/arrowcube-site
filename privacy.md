# Arrow Cube Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Arrow Cube is a mobile puzzle game for Android and iPhone published by Rubicon Performance LLC, a Nevada limited liability company ("Rubicon", "we", "us" or "our"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, who receives it, how long we keep it and the choices you have. The current version is always at https://rubiconperformance.github.io/arrowcube-site/privacy/.

Questions about this policy or your information: support-arrowcube@rubiconperform.com.

## 1. The short version

- You can play without creating an account. We never ask for your name, email address or phone number in the game.
- The game uses device identifiers, gameplay events, purchase records, crash reports and your approximate location (country or region, from your IP address) to run the game, fix problems, show ads and measure our advertising.
- We work with advertising, attribution and analytics partners, named in section 5. Some of them use this information for their own purposes, as their own policies explain.
- We do not sell your information for money. You can turn off personalized ads at any time in the game's Settings, and you can ask us to delete your information.
- In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent first, through Google's consent form when you start the game. On iPhone, the game asks Apple's permission before any partner can use your advertising identifier.

## 2. Information we collect

**Information the game keeps on your device.** Your progress, coins, boosters, settings, profile name, icon and frame, and the items you own are saved on your phone. Your profile name never leaves your device.

**Device and app identifiers.**
- A random player ID the game creates when you first open it.
- On Android: your device's advertising ID and app set ID, which Android provides to apps.
- On iPhone: the identifier for vendors, and the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when Apple's prompt asks.
- Identifiers our analytics, attribution and crash-reporting tools create for the app on your device.

**Gameplay and app activity.**
- The levels you start, win and fail, the boosters and rewards you use, the screens you open, how long you play, and the ads you see and tap.
- Settings you change, including your consent and personalized-ads choices.

**Purchases.** When you buy something, Google Play or the App Store sends us the product, price, currency, order or transaction number and a purchase token or receipt, so we can verify the purchase and give you what you bought. Google or Apple handles the payment: we never see your card or payment details.

**Diagnostics.** Crash reports, error types, start-up and loading times, frame rate, memory use, and your device model, operating system version and app version.

**Approximate location.** Your country or region, worked out from your IP address and from your phone's mobile network or store billing country. The game does not ask for or use your precise (GPS) location.

**Information you send us.** If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it to answer you.

## 3. How we use information

- **To run the game:** save and restore your progress and purchases, verify purchases, deliver rewards, set the difficulty of levels and adjust settings and offers remotely.
- **To understand and improve the game:** see which levels are too hard or too easy, where players stop, what crashes and what runs slowly.
- **For advertising:**
  - Show ads in the game, including rewarded ads you choose to watch.
  - Measure which of our own ads on other apps and websites led to an install or a purchase, and help those platforms find players who will enjoy the game.
- **To keep the game fair and secure:** detect fraud, fake purchases and invalid ad traffic.
- **To tailor the game to how you play:** for example, the difficulty of upcoming levels and which offers you see.
- **To meet legal obligations:** for example, keeping purchase records for tax and accounting.

## 4. Advertising

- **Who serves the ads:** the game shows ads through AppLovin MAX. MAX picks each ad from AppLovin and from the advertising networks listed in section 5: Unity Ads, InMobi, Pangle, BIGO Ads, Mintegral and Moloco.
- **What those networks receive:** your advertising ID (on iPhone only if you allow tracking), IP address, device information and ad interactions. They use these to choose and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
- **Measuring our own ads:** when we advertise Arrow Cube on other apps and websites, for example on Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google and AppLovin, we use AppsFlyer to learn which ads led to installs, purchases and in-game milestones. AppsFlyer sends those results, with an advertising ID or campaign ID, to the platform that showed the ad.
- **On iPhone:** the game shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any partner reads your advertising identifier. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", no partner receives it.
- **Personalized ads:** these use information about your activity to choose ads for you. You can turn them off (section 7); you will still see ads, but they will be less tailored to you.
- **No Ads purchase:** No Ads removes forced ads. Rewarded ads stay available if you choose to watch them.

## 6. Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you play in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form when you first start it.
- **What needs your consent:** we and our partners use your information for personalized ads, and for the measurement and analytics services named in the form, only if you agree.
- **Changing your choice:** any time, in Settings > Privacy and consent.

**Our legal bases:**
- **Performance of our contract with you:** providing the game, your purchases and restores.
- **Our legitimate interests:** keeping the game working and secure, fixing crashes, preventing fraud and understanding how the game is played.
- **Your consent:** personalized ads and the consent-based services above.
- **Legal obligation:** for example, keeping purchase records.

## 7. Your choices and rights

- **Personalized ads:** turn them off in Settings > Privacy and consent > Personalized ads. We then tell AppLovin and the networks bidding through it not to personalize ads for you, and we limit what we send to advertising platforms.
- **Advertising ID:**
  - On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your phone).
  - On iPhone, you can allow or stop tracking for Arrow Cube at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
- **Notifications:** turn game notifications off in Settings, or in your phone's settings. Notifications are scheduled on your phone; no information is sent to schedule them.
- **Access, correction, deletion and objection:** email support-arrowcube@rubiconperform.com.
  - Settings > Contact Us opens an email with your player ID already in it. We need that ID to find your information, because we do not know your name or email address.
  - The steps are also at https://rubiconperformance.github.io/arrowcube-site/delete-data/.
  - We answer within 30 days.
  - We delete or anonymize your information, and ask the partners that hold it on our behalf to do the same.
  - Uninstalling the game deletes what is stored on your phone, but not what was already sent to us or our partners; ask us for that.
- **Complaints:** if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

## 8. United States privacy rights

Depending on the state you live in, you may have the right to:
- know what personal information we collect and how we use it;
- get a copy of it;
- correct it or delete it;
- opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising.

**What we collect, in the terms these laws use:**
- identifiers (player ID, advertising ID);
- commercial information (purchase history);
- internet or other electronic network activity (gameplay and ad interactions);
- approximate geolocation;
- device and diagnostic information.

**Sale and sharing.** We do not sell personal information for money. Giving identifiers and in-game events to advertising partners to show and measure personalized ads can count as a "sale" or "sharing" under some state laws. To opt out, turn off Personalized ads in Settings > Privacy and consent, or reset or delete your advertising ID (on iPhone, turn off tracking for the game).

**Requests and non-discrimination.** To use your other rights, email support-arrowcube@rubiconperform.com. We will not treat you differently for using any of these rights.

## 9. How long we keep information

- **Analytics, diagnostics and crash reports:** no longer than 24 months, then deleted or anonymized.
- **Purchase records:** as long as tax and accounting law requires.
- **Emails you send us:** as long as we need them to help you, and no longer than 24 months after the conversation ends.
- **On your phone:** what the game stores there stays until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

## 10. Security

The game sends information only over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access is limited to the people and services that need it. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security, but we work to protect your information and to fix problems quickly.

## 11. International transfers

We and our partners process information in the United States, Singapore and other countries where our partners operate. When information from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and their UK and Swiss versions), or on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified.

## 12. Children

Arrow Cube is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the age of digital consent where you live. If you believe a child has used the game and we hold their information, contact us at support-arrowcube@rubiconperform.com and we will delete it.

## 13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is material, we also tell you in the game before it takes effect.
