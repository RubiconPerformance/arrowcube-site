# Delete your Arrow Cube data

Arrow Cube does not use accounts. To ask us to delete the information linked to your game install:

1. In the game, open **Settings** and copy your **player ID** (shown in Settings).
2. Email **support-arrowcube@rubiconperform.com** with the subject **Delete my data**, and include your player ID.

What happens next:

- We delete or anonymize the information linked to that player ID, and ask the partners that hold it on our behalf to do the same, as described in our [privacy policy](../privacy/).
- Purchase records we must keep by law (for example, for tax) are kept only as long as the law requires.
- Uninstalling the game deletes what is stored on your phone, but not what was already sent to us or our partners. Email us for that.

You can also reset your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings.
