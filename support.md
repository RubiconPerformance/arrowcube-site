# Arrow Cube support

Questions, problems, purchases or feedback: email **support-arrowcube@rubiconperform.com**.

The quickest way is in the game: open **Settings** and tap **Contact Us**. An email opens with your player ID already in the subject, so we can find your game without asking for your name.

## Purchases

- If something you bought did not arrive, open **Settings** and tap **Restore purchases**.
- Still missing? Email us with the order number from your Google Play or App Store receipt.

## Privacy and your data

- Read our [privacy policy](../privacy/).
- To delete your data, follow the steps on [Delete your Arrow Cube data](../delete-data/).
- To turn off personalized ads, open **Settings** > **Privacy and consent** in the game.
