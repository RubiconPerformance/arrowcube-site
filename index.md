# Arrow Cube

Arrow Cube is a 3D arrow puzzle by Rubicon Performance LLC. Tap an arrow whose path is clear and it flies away. Clear every arrow to win.

## Support

Questions, problems or feedback: **support-arrowcube@rubiconperform.com**

The quickest way is **Settings** > **Contact Us** in the game: the email it opens already carries your player ID. More on the [support page](support/).
